# 2026 Complete Sanya Travel Guide

## 1. Destination Overview
Sanya sits at the southernmost tip of Hainan Province and is China's only tropical coastal resort city. It stays warm year-round, making it the country's top winter escape from the cold. Sanya is famous for its turquoise seas, white-sand beaches, and upscale resort hotels, with Yalong Bay, Haitang Bay, and Sanya Bay each offering a distinct flavor. It's well suited to beach getaways, family trips, honeymoons, and water-sports enthusiasts.

## 2. Top Attractions (with Admission and Location Info)

### 2.1 Yalong Bay
* **Location**: Yalong Bay National Resort District, Jiyang District, Sanya
* **Admission**: Beach access free; central plaza 50 yuan/person
* **Suggested Duration**: Half a day to a full day
* **Overview**: Known as "the world's number one bay," Yalong Bay has crystal-clear water and fine white sand, making it one of Sanya's best bays. Luxury hotels line the shore, so it's ideal for lounging at a resort and relaxing on the beach. Water sports are plentiful, including diving, jet skiing, and banana boating.

### 2.2 Wuzhizhou Island
* **Location**: Haitang District, Sanya (reached by boat from the Wuzhizhou Island Pier)
* **Admission**: 144 yuan/person (round-trip ferry included)
* **Suggested Duration**: A full day
* **Overview**: Sanya's most beautiful offshore island, with exceptionally clear water that makes it one of China's top diving destinations. On-island highlights include Lovers' Bridge, Sunrise-Viewing Rock, and the Mazu Temple. It's more economical to buy a combo ticket covering multiple water activities. Book ahead during peak season.

### 2.3 The End of the Earth (Tianya Haijiao)
* **Location**: Tianya Town, Tianya District, Sanya
* **Admission**: Free (admission has been waived since 2023)
* **Suggested Duration**: 2-3 hours
* **Overview**: One of Sanya's cultural landmarks, named for two massive boulders inscribed with "Tianya" (edge of the sky) and "Haijiao" (corner of the sea). While locals sometimes joke it's "just a few rocks," it remains a classic photo spot with its own charm. Sunset offers the best light for photos.

### 2.4 Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone
* **Location**: Nanshan Village, Yazhou District, Sanya (about a 40-minute drive from downtown)
* **Admission**: 129 yuan/person
* **Suggested Duration**: Half a day to a full day
* **Overview**: A national 5A-rated scenic area, home to the awe-inspiring 108-meter Guanyin of the South Sea statue rising from the ocean. The site also includes Nanshan Temple, the Buddhist Gate of Non-Duality, and Longevity Valley. The vegetarian cuisine at the temple's restaurant is worth trying. Great for travelers interested in Buddhist culture and scenic nature.

### 2.5 Atlantis Water Park
* **Location**: Haitang North Road, Haitang District, Sanya
* **Admission**: 298 yuan/person (free for hotel guests)
* **Suggested Duration**: A full day
* **Overview**: A large water park with thrilling attractions like Poseidon's Fury, a high-speed lazy river, and a wave pool. The adjacent aquarium lets you view marine life. Excellent for families with kids — plan to spend the whole day.

## 3. Local Food and Budget Guide

* **Seafood Feast**: A Sanya must-try. Buy fresh seafood at the First Market and have it cooked at a nearby processing stall; budget **150-300 yuan per person**.
* **Wenchang Chicken**: The most famous of Hainan's "four classic dishes," known for its smooth skin and tender meat; budget **60-80 yuan per person**.
* **Baoluo Rice Noodles**: A Hainan specialty noodle dish with a flavorful broth; budget **15-20 yuan per person**.
* **Qingbuliang**: A Hainan specialty dessert of coconut milk topped with assorted grains and fruit; budget **15-25 yuan per person**.
* **Coconut Chicken Hot Pot**: A hot pot with a sweet, savory coconut-water broth; budget **100-150 yuan per person**.
* **Mango Rice Rolls**: A trendy Sanya dessert combining mango with rice-noodle rolls; budget **15-20 yuan per person**.

## 4. Where to Stay

* **Yalong Bay**: Home to Sanya's cluster of luxury resorts and the best beach quality — ideal for a hotel-focused stay. Hotel budget: **800-3,000 yuan/night**.
* **Haitang Bay**: A newer development area anchored by top-tier hotels like Atlantis, well suited for families. Hotel budget: **600-5,000 yuan/night**.
* **Sanya Bay**: Close to downtown and The End of the Earth, offering the best value for money — good for budget-conscious travelers. Hotel budget: **200-600 yuan/night**.
* **Dadonghai**: Convenient transport and abundant dining options, popular with younger travelers. Hotel budget: **300-800 yuan/night**.

## 5. Sample Classic 4-Day Itinerary (Agent Extraction Sample)

* **Day 1: Arrival and Beach Time**
  * Morning: Arrive in Sanya and check into a hotel in Yalong Bay or Haitang Bay.
  * Afternoon: Relax on the hotel beach and try water activities (snorkeling, jet skiing).
  * Evening: Enjoy a seafood buffet at the hotel or head to the First Market for fresh seafood.
* **Day 2: Wuzhizhou Island**
  * Morning: Take a boat to **Wuzhizhou Island** for diving or an underwater walk.
  * Afternoon: Tour the island and check out Lovers' Bridge and Sunrise-Viewing Rock.
  * Evening: Return to the hotel to unwind at the pool or spa.
* **Day 3: Culture and Nature**
  * Morning: Visit the **Nanshan Cultural Tourism Zone** and see the 108-meter Guanyin statue.
  * Afternoon: Sample the vegetarian cuisine at Nanshan Temple and visit the Gate of Non-Duality.
  * Evening: Watch the sunset at **The End of the Earth**, a classic photo stop.
* **Day 4: Family Fun or Leisure**
  * Morning: Head to **Atlantis Water Park** for the water slides and wave pool.
  * Afternoon: Visit the aquarium and watch a marine life show.
  * Evening: Shop at the Haitang Bay duty-free mall to wrap up the trip.
